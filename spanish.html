Spanish

Our Spanish class is taught by Miss Maestra, who is a very energetic and fun person
to be around. She comes up with fun activities and sentences to use in the
classroom, tells us how to sign words in Spanish, uses funny stories to
teach us, and comes up with fun games. Her class is more like a gift and
something new since I never had a Spanish class at my old school, and I'm
glad I finally get the opportunity to learn.

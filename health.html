the recipe and ingredients to make one of these healthy bars

  1. Ingredient Amount
  2. Granola 3 tbsp
  3. Almond butter 1 tbsp
  4. Almonds, chopped 1 tbsp
  5. Dried strawberries, chopped 1 tbsp
  6. Dark chocolate, chopped/chips 1 1/2 tsp
  7. Coconut oil 1/2 tsp

Preheat your oven to 325°F (165°C).Mix the granola, almonds, and dried strawberries in a small bowl.Warm
the almond butter and coconut oil until they're smooth.Pour them over the dry ingredients and mix well.
Gently mix in the dark chocolate.Press the mixture very firmly into a small, parchment-lined oven-safe dish
or shape it into a compact bar on a small baking sheet.Bake for 12–15 minutes, until lightly golden around
the edges.Let it cool completely before removing it from the pan.
